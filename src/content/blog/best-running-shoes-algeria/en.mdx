Shoes can change how running *feels*, but no model can guarantee protection from injury. Prioritize a secure, comfortable fit with enough room for your toes. In Algeria, current stock, sizing, warranty, and import cost often matter more than online hype.

This guide is a shortlist to compare, not a claim that every size is currently available. Some models may be sold locally, while others require an importer or international seller. Confirm delivery, returns, authenticity, and final price before ordering.

<Callout type="tip" title="How to pick, in one line">
For your first pair, prioritise a **cushioned daily trainer in your correct size** over any specific brand or hype model. Comfort and fit beat marketing every time.
</Callout>

## How to buy the right size

- **Measure late in the day**, when your feet are at their largest.
- Leave roughly **a thumb's width** in front of your longest toe, then follow the manufacturer's size chart. Do not assume every brand requires half a size more.
- Your heel should lock in place with no slipping; the toe box should let your toes wiggle.
- If you can, **try before a run, not after sitting all day** — feet swell when you run.
